The Immortal Gods

The universe is animated by sovereign divine forces. We honor the deities through classical ritual precision, bloodless offerings, and the embodiment of the 21 virtues in daily life.

Become a Cultor

Walking The Virtuous Path to Elysium is an active, living commitment to the immortal gods through daily virtue and household piety.

The Three Commitments of a Cultor:

  • Embody the 21 Virtues: Strive daily for justice, truth, temperance, and benevolence in thought, word, and deed.
  • Maintain the Sacred Hearth (Lararium): Honor Janus, the household Lares, and the Olympian deities with bloodless offerings (pure water, wine, honey, incense, spelt).
  • Civic Public Service: Demonstrate the faith through quiet public kindness, ecological respect, and community responsibility.

Consult our foundational scripture, Ritus, for daily prayer formulas and altar construction guidelines.
